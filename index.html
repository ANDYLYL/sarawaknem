HOW IT WORKS

The Sarawak Energy 2-Meter NEM System

Net Energy Metering (NEM) lets your rooftop solar work like a bank account. It runs as two separate systems: your solar exports 100% of its output to SESCO through the PV Meter, while your home keeps taking its supply from the grid through the Grid Meter. Your bill is the difference.

1

Generate

PV panels convert sunlight into DC power. The inverter converts it to grid-synchronised AC.

2

Export 100%

All solar output goes straight to the SESCO grid. The PV Meter records every kWh exported as credit.

3

Home stays on grid

Your house load is a separate circuit, supplied from the grid through the Grid Meter, day and night.

4

Net off monthly

At night PV generation stops. On your bill, the PV Meter export is credited against the Grid Meter import.

METER 1

Grid Meter (Import)

Your existing SESCO meter (or the smart meter that replaces it). It measures all the electricity your home uses, day and night, because the house load is always supplied from the grid.

  • • Stays in place, no change to your account
  • • Billed at the normal domestic tariff
METER 2

PV Meter (Export)

A dedicated meter added for your solar system. It measures 100% of your solar output exported to the grid, which is credited against your import.

  • • Installed within 1 metre of the Sarawak Energy meter, labelled "PV METER"
  • • Metering fee: RM 500 (single-phase) / RM 1,000 (three-phase)
Key NEM rules (Sarawak Energy): single-phase systems up to 10 kWac, three-phase up to 30 kWac; system output should not exceed 75% of your average consumption over the past 3 months; the PV system must match your supply phase; battery storage is generally not permitted; connections are subject to quota and transformer capacity. Installers must hold EIU GCPV Designer and Chargeman certifications.
